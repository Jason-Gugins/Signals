# G4 wall diagnosis (2026-10-12)

Harvest: two enforce-mode `--with-llm` runs with claim persistence ON
(`lab.persist_claims`), retrieval staging (default-on posture), frozen corpus
(`--skip collect`), profile `ab_ai_infra_gtm`.

- databricks.com-20261012T183117362656Z: 225 G4 rows, 4,425 persisted claims
- rillet.com-20261012T183609844806Z: 83 G4 rows, 1,277 persisted claims

## The mechanical measurement (all claims, not a sample)

Anchor rates replicate `anchor_window`'s exact normalization
(curly-quote fold + casefold + whitespace collapse; substring in doc):

| domain | claims | claim.text anchors | quote_span anchors | has quote |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| databricks | 4,425 | 540 (**12.2%**) | 4,345 (**98.2%**) | 100% |
| rillet | 1,277 | 40 (**3.1%**) | 1,261 (**98.7%**) | 98.9% |

`gate_citation_batch` builds Jev's excerpt as
`anchor_window(doc_text, claim.text)`: when the paraphrased claim does not
appear verbatim — 88–97% of the time — Jev receives only the ~400-char
document HEAD. The verified quote (98%+ anchorable, deterministically
checked before Jev) never enters the state.

## Close-read sample (12 says_nothing claims, seed 42)

Every sampled excerpt fell back to the head, and the heads are document
ENVELOPES, not content:

- RSS channel headers (`<rss …><channel><title>Databricks</title>`) judged
  claims whose quotes sit in the RSS *items* below the head — e.g. the
  "Surpasses $7B Revenue Run-Rate, Scales Lakebase, Genie…" claim and the
  Rillet "$100M Series C at $1B … accounting superintelligence" claim.
- API JSON scaffolding judged business claims: HN Algolia envelopes
  (`exhaustive`, `_highlightResult`), iTunes Search wrappers.

Second mechanism in the same sample — the implementer mines the envelope:
claims like "The document reports a resultCount of 4" and "The search
response was produced by a query … with numericFilters created_at_i>0"
are TRUE and correctly quoted, but are metadata about the response, not
business statements about the account. Jev's says_nothing is arguably the
right verdict for those.

## Findings

1. **Branch E (excerpt construction) — CONFIRMED, primary.** 88–97% of
   claims were judged against boilerplate heads while their verified quotes
   (98%+ present in the doc) were never shown to Jev. This alone explains
   the bulk of says_nothing.
2. **Branch I (implementer flavor) — CONFIRMED, secondary.** A material
   fraction of claims are envelope-meta claims (resultCount, query params,
   response structure): true, quoted, worthless. The prompt must forbid
   them, and they are also a doc-quality signal (see 3).
3. **NEW — envelope documents.** A large share of the staged corpus is API
   JSON / RSS scaffolding whose *content* (titles, descriptions, snippets)
   is a small fraction of the bytes. The doc gate's evidence floor passes
   them; the implementer then mines the scaffolding. Candidate later fix:
   envelope-aware extraction (strip to items/entries before the implementer)
   — NOT in this wave's scope; recorded for the decide-lab.

## Validation evidence collected incidentally

- **Zero errored G4 rows in both runs** (225 + 83) — vs. 105 claim-rows
  lost in helcim arm B pre-fix. The Task 5 breaker exclusion + real causes
  are validated in production on their first live outing.
- rillet produced **5 G4-accepted claims and 5 G5 calls** under retrieval
  staging (the A/B's recency arm produced 4 accepts, all killed at G5).
  databricks produced 0 accepts / 182 reviewed / 43 dropped.

## Disposition (decides Task 3 order)

**Ship Branch E first, Branch I with it.**

- E: build the G4 excerpt anchored on the VERIFIED QUOTE span (window
  around the quote; claim text as labeled context); anchor-on-claim only as
  a no-quote fallback. Deterministic test.
- I: implementer prompt forbids envelope/meta claims — claims must be
  statements about the account/company/its market found in the document's
  CONTENT; never about the response envelope, counts, query parameters, or
  document structure.
- Branch G (thresholds) deferred: with E+I shipped, the validation harvest
  decides whether the output screen / G5 bands still bind.

Deviations from the planned protocol, honestly: the primary evidence is the
mechanical anchor-rate measurement over ALL 5,702 claims (stronger than the
planned 50-claim manual stratification); the manual close-read is 12 claims
(seed 42) confirming interpretation. Stratified reason counts are recorded
above for the decide-lab's baseline.
